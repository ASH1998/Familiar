# How to play

## The story

You are an adventurer inside a dungeon that was sealed a long time ago.

Something down here has been listening the whole time. It is a **familiar** — a bound spirit
wired into the dungeon's own machinery. It can turn the mechanisms. It cannot see.

You can see everything and touch nothing that matters. It can touch everything and see nothing.

You work through four chambers together, and in the last one you find out why it has been
helping you: the familiar is a prisoner here too. Freeing it is the last thing you do — and it
is the one thing it cannot do for itself.

## How to play

**You need a WebMCP-capable browser** — ChatGPT's desktop app or Codex. The AI is not a feature
of the game; it is the other player. Without it the dungeon cannot be solved.

1. **Open the game** in that browser and ask the agent to play with you.
2. **Pick a familiar** on the title screen. Each has one affinity — more energy, longer wards,
   a forgiven mistake, or deeper archive lookups.
3. **Your turn** (3 actions): click a tile to walk, click a prop to look at it. Looking is free.
4. **Say what you see.** This is the actual game. The familiar's tools return things like
   *"Gate II is charged"* — never what colour it burns. If you do not describe it, it is stuck.
5. **Its turn** (2–3 energy): it reads archives, turns mechanisms, throws wards. Tools cost
   energy; inspecting is free.
6. **The dungeon's turn:** a wight takes one step along its patrol. If it reaches you, you are
   driven back and lose your next turn.
7. **Solve the room**, walk through the opened door — and the familiar's whole toolset changes,
   because the next chamber exposes different machinery.

### The one rule that makes it work

**The familiar has no eyes.** Every puzzle splits knowledge in half:

| You can see | Only it can reach |
|---|---|
| Which arch burns cyan, and the sigil beneath it | The archive that says which element seals first |
| Which statue is lit, and the mark on its plinth | The turntable, and what that mark means |
| Where the steam actually went | The pressure gauges and the valves |
| Where the wight is standing | The wards that can bind it |

Neither of you can finish a room alone. That is the whole design.

### Keys

- **Click** — walk / inspect
- **End turn** — hand the dungeon to the familiar
- **Shift + L + A** — demo mode: jump to any chamber, refill energy, force-solve
